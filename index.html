OUR VISION

Organize your daily activities, stay productive, and keep track of your prayer schedule easily.

How to Use This Website

This website helps users organize their daily tasks and keep track of prayer schedules.

  1. Navigate using the menu bar at the top of the page.
  2. Click on the Tasks section to add your daily activities.
  3. Enter a task in the input field and press the Add button.
  4. Click on a task to mark it as completed.
  5. Use the delete button to remove completed or unwanted tasks.
  6. Visit the Prayer section to view daily prayer reminders.
